Race Management

We manage all races and practices through a shared Google spreadsheet. Once someone is officially part of the crew, they're given access to the sheet. Crew members are assigned qualifications for specific positions, and those qualifications determine which roles they can sign up for.

For each race or practice, crew members select themselves into available positions directly in the spreadsheet. Below is a read only view of that spreadsheet.
