# Kite Set (Asymmetric)

## Rigging (Dock or Before Race)

- [ ] Choose kite for conditions
- [ ] Bring kite on deck — attach bag to rail
- [ ] Attach sheets — run through aft-most blocks (slightly forward blocks for larger kite)
- [ ] Attach tack line to tack — **tack goes over the sheet** (required for inside gybes)
- [ ] Confirm which halyard (port or stbd) — attach to head
- [ ] Confirm which tack line (port or stbd) — rig to bowsprit end
- [ ] Two halyards rigged (port & stbd), two tack lines rigged (port & stbd) — ready for peeling
- [ ] **Typical port rounding (starboard gybe, kite to port):** use port halyard & port tack line

## Pre-Race Test Hoist
- [ ] Hoist kite out of the bag
- [ ] Set and sail briefly — confirm rigging is correct
- [ ] Douse kite
- [ ] Feed kite into bow hatch **head first** — sheets and tack line remain attached
- [ ] Kite is now ready for race hoist

## At the Mark — Hoist

### Prepare (Before Rounding)
- [ ] Sheets led and clear
- [ ] Pit crew: in position at halyard
- [ ] Mast crew: in position for sweating
- [ ] Bowsprit ready to deploy

### Execute
- [ ] Driver or tactician calls hoist during mark rounding
- [ ] Deploy bowsprit
- [ ] Pit & mast: hoist halyard — hand-over-hand fast
- [ ] Let kite fly — **do not trim until at the top**
- [ ] Confirm kite is fully hoisted
- [ ] Trim sheet once at the top

### After Set
- [ ] Driver or tactician calls **"Jib down"** — timing depends on conditions
- [ ] Lower and stow jib
