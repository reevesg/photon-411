# Gybing

## Roles
- **Skipper** — calls the gybe
- **Main trimmer** — controls boom across; stands behind boom in light air
- **Runner (port & stbd)** — one per side
- **Jib trimmer** — rodeo: holds both sheets at leeward cabin top winch
- **Bowperson** — guides clew, runs new sheet back

## Sequence (Inside Gybe)

### Prepare
- [ ] Skipper: **"Ready to gybe?"**
- [ ] Crew: **"Ready"**
- [ ] Lazy jib sheet brought up to leeward cabin top winch — trimmer holds both sheets (rodeo)
- [ ] Bowperson: positions at bow, grabs lazy sheet
- [ ] Runner crew: ready to swap as boom crosses

### Execute
- [ ] Skipper: bears away downwind
- [ ] Bowperson: pulls clew forward through inside of forestay
- [ ] Main trimmer: brings main to center in breeze; guides by hand in light air
- [ ] Skipper: **"Gybing"** — boom crosses
- [ ] As main comes over: ease old windward runner, set new windward runner
- [ ] Bowperson: runs new sheet back to cockpit
- [ ] Jib trimmer: releases old sheet, trims new sheet

### Trim
- [ ] Trim main & vang on new gybe
- [ ] Confirm runners — windward set, leeward eased
- [ ] Trim jib to new tack

## Outside Gybe
Use in heavy air when an inside gybe is too risky.
