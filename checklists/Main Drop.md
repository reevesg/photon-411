# Main Drop

> Used any time the main needs to come down — mid-race, post-race, or at the dock. Procedure is the same.

## Key Principles
- **The boom will swing freely and dangerously without the main up — this is one of the most dangerous maneuvers on the boat**
- A preventer must be in place before the main comes down
- Driver keeps boat pointed into the wind throughout — do not deviate

---

## Prepare
- [ ] Driver: head into the wind and hold there
- [ ] Ease vang, cunningham, mainsheet
- [ ] **Bring on both running backstays — both tensioned**
- [ ] **Attach preventer from boom end to running backstays — secure firmly**
- [ ] Confirm preventer is holding boom before touching halyard

## Drop
- [ ] **Advanced:** Once into the wind, drive the boat backwards to reduce apparent wind to near zero — makes the drop significantly easier. Requires practice.
- [ ] Ease main halyard from inside cabin at base of mast — controlled
- [ ] Multiple crew flake the sail onto the boom:
  - One crew on leech
  - One crew on luff
  - Additional crew in between
- [ ] Driver: ease slightly across the wind port and starboard to encourage alternating flakes to port then starboard
- [ ] Continue until sail is fully down and flaked on boom

## Secure
- [ ] Lash sail to boom with sail ties
- [ ] Coil and secure main halyard
- [ ] Cover goes on at dock or mooring

## At the Dock/Mooring
- [ ] Put main cover on
- [ ] Ease running backstays to neutral tension
