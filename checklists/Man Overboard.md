# Man Overboard

## Immediate
- [ ] First person to see: **shout "MAN OVERBOARD — [PORT/STARBOARD]"** as loud as possible
- [ ] That person becomes **spotter — point at victim, never look away, never leave this role**
- [ ] Press **MOB button on Garmin chartplotter**
- [ ] Stay calm — a clean recovery depends on it

---

## Recovery — Jib Up (Quick Stop)
- [ ] **Tack immediately — do not release the jib** — backed jib stops the boat
- [ ] Drop the jib
- [ ] Start engine
- [ ] Motor around to the victim
- [ ] Throw life sling — circle around victim to wrap the sling line around them
- [ ] Victim grabs line and is secured in life sling
- [ ] Neutral — bring victim to stern using life sling line
- [ ] Bring victim aboard at the stern

---

## Recovery — Kite Up
- [ ] **Bear away immediately**
- [ ] Douse kite using standard douse procedure — **stay calm, do the job cleanly**
- [ ] Start engine
- [ ] Motor around to victim
- [ ] Throw life sling — circle to wrap line around victim
- [ ] Neutral — bring victim to stern using life sling line
- [ ] Bring victim aboard at the stern

---

## After Recovery
- [ ] Account for all crew — confirm headcount
- [ ] Assess victim — first aid if needed
- [ ] VHF Ch 16 — call for assistance if required
- [ ] Log incident

> Spotter role is sacred — do not leave it for any reason until the victim is back on board.
