# Jib Trim

## Controls
- **Car position** — sets twist and the relationship between luff and leech tension
- **Halyard** — luff tension, draft position
- **Sheet** — overall tension, leech tension, power

---

## Setup Sequence (Upwind)
1. **Car** — set twist via leech telltales (see below)
2. **Halyard** — set luff tension for conditions
3. **Sheet** — trim to luff telltales

> After moving the car, always re-trim the sheet.

---

## What to Look For

### Luff Telltales (Primary — sheet & angle)
Use luff telltales to optimize sheet tension and steering angle.

| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Windward telltale just lifting | On the edge — ideal VMG angle | Hold |
| Windward telltale fully stalling | Pinching too much | Bear away or ease sheet |
| Leeward telltale stalling | Over-trimmed or footing | Head up or ease sheet |
| Both streaming aft | Good trim | Hold |

### Leech Telltales (Car Position — twist)
Use leech telltales to set car position.

| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Upper leech telltale stalling | Too much leech tension, not enough twist | Move car aft or ease sheet |
| Upper leech telltale always streaming | Too much twist | Move car forward |
| All leech telltales just flowing | Correct twist | Hold |

### Luff (Halyard Tension)
| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal creases from luff | Too much halyard tension | Ease halyard |
| Diagonal crease from luff toward clew | Draft too far aft | Add halyard tension |
| Smooth luff, draft ~40% back | Correct | Hold |

---

## Condition Reference

### Light Air
- Car: forward — fuller entry, more power
- Halyard: light tension
- Sheet: eased — allow twist, keep luff telltales just lifting

### Medium Air
- Car: mid position
- Halyard: moderate tension
- Sheet: firm — leech telltales just flowing

### Heavy Air
- Car: aft — open leech, depower
- Halyard: firm — pull draft forward
- Sheet: firm but allow upper leech to twist off
- Consider headsail change if overpowered
