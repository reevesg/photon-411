# Kite Douse (Asymmetric)

## Plan Early
- [ ] Discuss douse timing well before the mark — agree on distance
- [ ] Agree on douse method: **bow hatch** or **companionway**
- [ ] Squirrel positions below at chosen hatch, ready to pull lazy sheet

## Key Principles
- **Ease, don't blow the sheet** — blowing sends the kite forward uncontrolled and risks a forestay wrap. Ease to depower and let it flag.
- **Tack vs halyard first determines where the kite goes:**
  - Release tack first → kite streams aft → use for companionway douse
  - Ease halyard first (keep tack) → kite stays at bow → use for bow hatch douse

---

## Bow Hatch Douse

### Prepare
- [ ] Bowperson: positions at bow hatch, opens hatch
- [ ] Squirrel: below at bow hatch, ready on lazy sheet
- [ ] Pit: ready on halyard

### Execute
- [ ] Bear away to depower kite
- [ ] **Ease sheet** — let kite flag, do not blow
- [ ] **Keep tack attached**
- [ ] Ease halyard in a controlled drop — bowperson feeds kite into hatch
- [ ] Squirrel pulls kite below from lazy sheet
- [ ] Bowperson and squirrel work together until kite is fully below

### After
- [ ] Close bow hatch
- [ ] Halyard secured and coiled
- [ ] Retract bowsprit
- [ ] Hoist jib as called

---

## Companionway Douse

### Prepare
- [ ] Squirrel: below at companionway, ready on lazy sheet
- [ ] Pit: ready on halyard
- [ ] Bowperson: on deck ready to assist

### Execute
- [ ] Bear away to depower kite
- [ ] **Ease sheet** — let kite flag, do not blow
- [ ] **Release tack** — kite streams aft toward boat
- [ ] **Drop halyard fast** — squirrel pulls kite in through companionway
- [ ] Bowperson assists feeding from deck if needed

### After
- [ ] Halyard secured and coiled
- [ ] Retract bowsprit
- [ ] Hoist jib as called
