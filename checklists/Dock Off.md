# Dock Off

## At the Dock
- [ ] Remove mainsail cover
- [ ] Rig headsail — confirm sail selection with skipper
- [ ] Rig kite — confirm with skipper
- [ ] Disconnect shore power
- [ ] Turn on instruments & chartplotter (Garmin breakers)
- [ ] Check bilge — pump if needed

## Departure
- [ ] All hands on deck — lines ready
- [ ] Cast off dock lines
- [ ] Motor to starting area
