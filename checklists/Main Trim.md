# Main Trim

## Setup Sequence (Upwind)
Set controls in this order — rig shape first, then sail shape, then fine tune.

1. **Runners** — set mast bend. More bend = flatter sail, open leech. Less = fuller, tighter leech.
2. **Checkstays** — fine tune lower mast bend and lower draft
3. **Halyard** — baseline luff tension
4. **Cunningham** — draft position fore/aft
5. **Outhaul** — foot depth
6. **Mainsheet** — leech tension and twist
7. **Traveler** — angle of attack

---

## What to Look For

### Leech Telltales
| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| All flowing slightly | Leech is open — good | Hold |
| Top telltale stalling (not flowing) | Leech too tight | Ease sheet or drop traveler |
| All telltales streaming hard | Too much twist, leech too open | Sheet on or raise traveler |
| Leech hooking to windward | Overbent, too much mainsheet | Ease sheet, check runners |

### Luff
| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Horizontal creases from luff | Cunningham over-tensioned | Ease cunningham |
| Diagonal crease from luff toward clew | Draft too far aft, need more luff tension | Add cunningham |
| Vertical creases along luff | Halyard too loose | Tension halyard |
| Smooth luff, draft ~40-45% back | Correct | Hold |

### Foot
| What you see | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Wrinkles along foot/boom | Outhaul too loose | Tension outhaul |
| Foot very flat, no depth | Outhaul too tight | Ease outhaul |

### Draft Position
- Target: draft **40–45% back** from luff in most conditions
- Draft too far forward (full at luff) → ease cunningham, ease runners slightly
- Draft too far aft (full at leech) → add cunningham, add runners

### Boat Behaviour
| What you feel | What it means | Adjust |
|---|---|---|
| Boat heeling hard, hard on helm | Overpowered | Add runners, cunningham, ease traveler, consider reef |
| Pointing well, good speed | Balanced | Hold |
| Boat feels slow, not pointing | Underpowered or too much twist | Sheet on, raise traveler, ease runners slightly |
| Light helm, boat feels loose | Too much twist or sail too flat | Sheet on, raise traveler |

## Condition Reference

### Light Air
- Runners: eased
- Checkstays: eased
- Cunningham: off
- Outhaul: slightly eased — some foot depth
- Mainsheet: eased for twist — top batten parallel to boom
- Traveler: up to centerline or above

### Medium Air
- Runners: moderate tension
- Checkstays: moderate
- Cunningham: light tension
- Outhaul: firm
- Mainsheet: firm — leech telltales just flowing
- Traveler: centered

### Heavy Air
- Runners: on hard
- Checkstays: on
- Cunningham: hard on — pull draft forward
- Outhaul: hard — flatten foot
- Mainsheet: firm but allow some twist to depower
- Traveler: drop to leeward
- Consider reef if helm is heavy and boat is overpowered
