# Headsail Change

## Key Principles
- Dual-groove headfoil — new sail always goes up in the **windward/inside groove**
- It is not possible to hoist in the leeward groove
- Always peel when racing to maintain speed — straight drop only when overpowered
- Two jib halyards (port & stbd) — confirm which is free before hoisting

---

## Peel (Racing)

### Prepare
- [ ] Select new sail for conditions
- [ ] Confirm which groove old sail is in
- [ ] **If old sail is in the windward groove — tack first** to make it the leeward groove and free the windward groove for the new sail
- [ ] Feed new sail bolt rope into windward (inside) groove at the base of the foil
- [ ] Attach second jib halyard to new sail head
- [ ] Attach sheets to new sail clew — lead outside shrouds, sheet to sheet

### Hoist
- [ ] Hoist new sail on second halyard — fast to the top
- [ ] Trim new sheet — confirm sail is filling and taking load
- [ ] **Do not touch old sail until new sail is genuinely flying**

### Douse Old Sail
- [ ] Ease old sheet
- [ ] Lower old halyard — controlled drop
- [ ] Remove old sail bolt rope from groove
- [ ] Bag and stow old sail

### After
- [ ] Trim new sail to target shape
- [ ] Coil and secure old halyard and sheets

---

## Straight Drop (Overpowered)
- [ ] Ease old sheet
- [ ] Lower old halyard — remove sail from groove
- [ ] Feed new sail into windward groove
- [ ] Attach halyard and sheets
- [ ] Hoist and trim
